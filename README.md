## Nejc Haberman

Computational biologist at Imperial College London (Department of Brain Sciences).
I work on RNA regulation in brain ageing and neurodegeneration, and on adapting
genomic foundation models to experimental data.

**Current work**
- Fine-tuning pretrained genomic foundation models (AlphaGenome) on functional
  genomics tracks — CAGE, Ribo-seq, RNA G-quadruplex — for variant effect prediction
- 3′UTR-derived capped RNAs (ucRNAs) in ageing and Alzheimer's disease
- Long-read transcriptomics, RNA structure, and multi-omic integration
